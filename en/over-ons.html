Ing. Steven Westhout (B-ICT)

Founder · Senior technical project manager
Network migration and infrastructure

His technical foundation was laid around 1999/2000 at Telfort and British Telecom, working on international Frame Relay and MPLS networks. From network engineering, that experience grew into leading complex technical projects.

At YAH-Advies, he is the fixed point of contact and the guardian of quality on every assignment. When a project needs more, he brings in specialists and suppliers from his network, with clear agreements on who delivers what.

Professional background: Bachelor of ICT, PRINCE2 Practitioner, ITIL, Scrum, Cisco CCNP and CCNA.
