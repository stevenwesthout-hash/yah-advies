Monuta — SD-WAN migration

Moving branches across the Netherlands, step by step, to a new SD-WAN network while services carry on.

View the project approach
Scale
A nationwide roll-out across 60 sites in the Netherlands, planned and migrated site by site.
Role / experience behind YAH-Advies
Directing the nationwide migration: planning, migrations and coordination between the branches and the IT suppliers.
Technology
SD-WAN, local network connections, patch cabinets and cabling; ServiceNow as the ITSM environment.
Parties
The IT suppliers KPN, Cegeka, Van Dorp and WL-ICT, and the branches themselves.
Approach
A fixed sequence for each site: preparation and cabling, activation of the connection, migration and sign-off.
Continuity
Migrations within agreed time windows, coordinated with each branch, so that funeral services continue without interruption.
Result / contribution
Ongoing programme; several branches have been migrated, with dashboards tracking migration progress.
