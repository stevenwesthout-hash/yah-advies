Impact analysis

Every technical change
affects the whole organisation.

A network migration is never just a technical matter. Using the SCOPAFIJTH model, we map out in advance which parts of the business are affected, from security to accommodation, so that nobody is caught off guard during delivery.

  • S
    SecurityWhat security risks arise, and how do access and data stay protected?
  • C
    Commercial & communicationWho needs to know what is changing, and when?
  • O
    OrganisationWhich roles, processes and responsibilities change?
  • P
    PersonnelWho is needed, and who needs training or onboarding?
  • A
    Administrative organisationWhich procedures, changes and records are involved?
  • F
    FinanceWhat does it cost, who pays and how is the budget monitored?
  • I
    Information managementWhich systems, data and integrations are affected?
  • J
    Legal (juridisch)Which contracts, SLAs and privacy agreements are relevant?
  • T
    TechnologyWhich technology changes, and what is the fallback route?
  • H
    Accommodation (huisvesting)Which sites, technical rooms and access arrangements are needed?

The aspects follow SCOPAFIJTH, the established Dutch framework for impact analysis; each letter stands for the Dutch term. The questions are our practical application of it in project control.
