The YAH-Advies ecosystem

Specialists and suppliers.
One point of control.

Not every project needs the same team. You keep one senior point of contact; around that, we assemble the specialists and suppliers each assignment requires.

Complex infrastructure projects are not delivered single-handedly. YAH-Advies brings together experienced technical specialists, suppliers and project professionals, and safeguards the coherence between technology, organisation, schedule, costs, risks and quality.

We work with a flexible network. For each assignment, we decide which people and parties are needed, what they deliver and how responsibilities are divided. Commitment and availability are agreed in advance.
